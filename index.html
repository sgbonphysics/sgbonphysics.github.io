About

My doctoral research focuses on precision spectral analysis of reactor and solar neutrinos and on searches for new interactions, within the JUNO and Borexino collaborations. In JUNO, I contributed the reactor antineutrino spectral fits and statistical analysis to the first measurement of the solar oscillation parameters, and I am analysing JUNO data on 8B solar neutrinos. In Borexino, I was a main contributor to the search for non-standard neutrino interactions with the full Phase-III data.

Research

Reactor antineutrino oscillations with the first JUNO data

JUNO Collaboration · 2021–now

Using its first 59.1 days of data, JUNO measured sin²θ12 and Δm²21, improving their precision by a factor of about 1.6 over all previous measurements combined. I performed the spectral fits and statistical analysis. Because the TAO reference spectrum was not yet available, I developed semi-model-independent fits that separate the uncertainties constrained by Daya Bay from the remaining model-dependent components, and showed that the latter shift the fitted parameters by less than 0.05σ.

Non-standard neutrino interactions in Borexino

Borexino Collaboration · 2022–now

I led the development of the fitting framework and the data analysis for the Borexino NSI search with the Phase-II and Phase-III data. A GPU-accelerated reweighting framework, 58× faster than a single CPU thread, made it possible to scan the full parameter space including the off-diagonal couplings. The analysis improved the flavour-diagonal constraints and extended Borexino's study to the full set of couplings.

8B solar neutrinos in JUNO

JUNO Collaboration · ongoing

I am developing the event selection and background model for the elastic-scattering and charged-current channels, aiming at the first observation of 8B solar neutrinos in JUNO. Most cosmogenic backgrounds are suppressed by 99.5% while a relatively high signal efficiency is retained.

In-situ measurement of the scintillator transparency

JUNO Collaboration

Using laser calibration data, I developed an optical model of the PMT occupancies to measure the attenuation length of the JUNO liquid scintillator in situ, providing a direct cross-check of the laboratory measurements.
